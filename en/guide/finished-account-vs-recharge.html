Pre-made accounts, top-ups and official billing compared

aiprice · snapshot 2026-11-15 15:43 UTC

“Is a pre-made account safe?” usually means “will it still work next week?” The three delivery models differ in who controls the account, not in price tier.

Pre-made account

You get credentials. The seller usually keeps the recovery mailbox, so they can take it back. Changing the password helps but does not remove that access. Best for short projects and bulk testing.

Top-up on your own account

You keep the account and the history; the seller completes the payment (region change, gift card, card). Risk sits in the payment leg: failed region changes, reclaimed cards, chargebacks that silently drop you back to the free tier.

Official subscription

The only tier with no intermediary. ~$20/month, and the account, history and support all belong to the vendor.

Three observable signals of a solid shop: catalogue breadth, multiple quote tiers per product (real price discovery), and after-sales text that specifies replacement windows.

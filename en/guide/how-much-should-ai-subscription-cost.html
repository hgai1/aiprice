What an AI subscription should actually cost in 2026

aiprice · snapshot 2026-11-15 15:43 UTC

The same ChatGPT Plus subscription is listed anywhere from ¥10 to ¥200 a month in the resale market. That spread is not random volatility: four genuinely different products share one keyword.

Four products, four price anchors

  • Official billing — your own account, charged by the vendor. Roughly the list price plus payment-channel cost. No account loss, full history.
  • Pre-made account — you receive a login that already has the plan. Cheapest tier; the seller may still control the recovery email.
  • Shared seat — one account split between several people. Lowest monthly cost, no privacy, can be kicked at any time.
  • Trial / eligibility accounts — short-term access, useful for validating a use case.

Comparing across these tiers produces nonsense conclusions. Fix the tier first, then compare prices inside it.

Duration is the real variable

A shared seat at ¥15 sounds unbeatable until you replace it three times in a month and re-feed every context window. For anything you use more than two months with history you care about, official billing wins on total cost.

Rule of thumb: if you would re-buy the same tier next month, buy the tier that never disappears.
